DETAILING / WATERPROOFING
GEN NOTES

1. SEE WATERPROOFING DESIGN DRAWINGS FOR WEATHER BARRIER, WATERPROOFING, FLASHINGS,
   SEALANTS, INSULATION AND ANY ADDITIONAL ASSOCIATED SCOPE.
2. SEPARATE STEEL FROM ANY ADJACENT METAL WITH SELF ADHERED FLASHING, TYP.
3. AIR SEALING PER 2021 IECC (R402.4.1.1) - ENSURE ALL SEALANT AND SPRAY FOAM AT
   INTERFACE GAPS, AND CRACKS BETWEEN DOOR/WINDOW AND AIR/WATER RESISTIVE BARRIER IS
   TIGHTLY SEALED TO STRUCTURE AND AIR TIGHT.
4. PAN FLASHING DAMS (BACK AND SIDE DAMS) UNDER WINDOW/DOOR UNITS TO EXTEND TO THE
   INTERIOR FACE OF WINDOW/DOOR UNIT AND NO FURTHER. FLOORING AND WINDOW SILLS AS
   DETAILED ARE TO TERMINATE DIRECTLY INTO BACK/SIDE DAM AND WINDOW/DOOR UNITS.
5. REFER TO DETAIL __/A6.__ FOR WINDOW SURROUNDS AT STONE AND VERTICAL WOOD BOARD SIDING.
