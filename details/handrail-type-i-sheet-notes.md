# Handrail / Guard Sheet Notes — Type I Non-Circular, Interior Stair

Companion to `handrail-detail-type-i.svg`. City of Aspen parcel: 2021 IBC as adopted and
amended, Group R-3, interior stair, handrail integral with the guard at the open side.

Drop notes 1–13 on the sheet as the general note block; keynotes **A–G** are on the
detail itself. Everything here is identical to the Type II version except **notes 6 and
7**, which are the two the profile change actually touches.

---

## GENERAL NOTES

**1. CODE BASIS.** All work per the 2021 International Building Code as adopted and
amended by City of Aspen Municipal Code Ch. 8.20. The 2021 IRC is **not adopted** by the
City — Aspen M.C. §8.16.010: *"The International Residential Code, 2021 Edition, will not
be adopted."* IBC §101.4.11 as amended by §8.20.020: *"All references to the International
Residential Code (IRC) within this code shall be deleted and the requirement of this code
as it pertains to one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses shall apply."* Occupancy is
Group R-3. Do not cite IRC R311 or R312 on this sheet.

**2. HANDRAILS REQUIRED.** Stairways shall have handrails on each side and comply with
§1014; stairways within dwelling units and spiral stairways are permitted to have a
handrail on one side only (§1011.11). Handrails serving flights of stairways shall be
adequate in strength and attachment in accordance with §1607.9 (§1014.1).

**3. HEIGHT.** Handrail height, measured above stair tread nosings, shall be uniform, not
less than **34"** and not more than **38"** (§1014.2). Where the top of the guard serves as
the handrail at the open side of a stair in a Group R-3 occupancy, the top of the guard
shall be 34" min to 38" max measured vertically from the line connecting the leading edges
of the treads (§1015.3 Exc. 3). Guards at landings and floors: not less than **36"** above
the adjacent walking surface for Group R-3 not more than three stories above grade
(§1015.3 Exc. 1). Base requirement absent those exceptions is 42" (§1015.3).

> ⚠ **VERIFY BEFORE ISSUE.** This office's City of Aspen sets have been drawn with **42"**
> guards. Aspen M.C. §8.20.020 amends §1015.3 only by *adding* Exception 9 (hot tubs, spas
> and pools), so base Exceptions 1–3 remain available and 36"/34–38" is defensible for
> R-3. Confirm with the Aspen Building Department, then dimension **one** height
> consistently across plans, sections, elevations and this detail.

**4. GUARDS — WHERE REQUIRED.** Per §1015.2 as amended by Aspen M.C. §8.20.020: *"Guards
shall be located along open-sided walking surfaces, including mezzanines, equipment
platforms, aisles, stairs, ramps, landings and adjacent to hot tubs, spas, and pools that
are located more than 30 inches (762 mm) measured vertically to the floor or grade below
at any point within 36 inches (914 mm) horizontally to the edge of the open side. Guards
shall be adequate in strength and attachment in accordance with Section 1607.9."*

**5. OPENING LIMITATIONS (§1015.4).** Required guards shall not have openings that allow
passage of a **4"** sphere from the walking surface to the required guard height.
Exceptions applicable here:
- Exc. 6 — within individual dwelling units in Group R-2 and R-3, guards on the open sides
  of stairs shall not allow passage of a **4⅜"** sphere.
- Exc. 2 — triangular openings at the open side of a stair formed by the riser, tread and
  bottom rail shall not allow passage of a **6"** sphere.
- Exc. 1 — from 36" to 42" above the walking surface, **4⅜"** sphere.

**6. GRASPABILITY — TYPE I NON-CIRCULAR (§1014.3.1).** *This is the note that changes.*
Required handrails shall comply with §1014.3.1 (Type I) or provide equivalent
graspability. For a **non-circular** Type I handrail: **perimeter not less than 4" and not
greater than 6¼"; maximum cross-sectional dimension 2¼"; minimum cross-sectional dimension
1"; edges 0.01" minimum radius.** (Circular Type I: 1¼"–2" outside diameter.)

The handrail on this detail is the **2" × 1⅛" cap only**:

| | Required | This profile |
|---|---|---|
| Perimeter | 4" min / 6¼" max | 6¼" milled square · **5.61" at R ⅜"** · 5.28" fully bullnosed |
| Max cross-sectional dimension | 2¼" | 2" |
| Min cross-sectional dimension | 1" | 1⅛" |
| Edge radius | 0.01" min | R ⅜" |

**No finger recess is required** — §1014.3.2 (Type II) applies only where the perimeter
exceeds 6¼".

> **Milled dead square this profile is exactly 6¼", which is the limit with zero
> tolerance. The R ⅜" edge easing is not decorative — it is what puts the perimeter
> safely inside the range. Carry the radius as a hard callout.**

**What counts toward the perimeter:** the graspable member only. The sub-rail below the
bottom of the rail is not part of the grip and is not measured. The same assembly read as
one 2" × 2⅜" section would exceed 6¼" and force Type II — so the bottom of the rail must
be unambiguous on the drawing.

**7. CONTINUITY AND OBSTRUCTIONS (§1014.4).** *This is the other note that changes.*
Handrail gripping surfaces shall be continuous, without interruption by newel posts or
other obstructions. Exceptions applicable within a dwelling unit: interruption by a newel
post at a turn or landing (Exc. 1); a volute, turnout, starting easing or starting newel
over the lowest tread (Exc. 2). Brackets or balusters attached to the bottom surface of
the handrail that do not project horizontally beyond the sides of the handrail within 1½"
of its bottom are not obstructions (Exc. 3); for each ½" of handrail perimeter above 4",
the 1½" vertical clearance may be reduced by ⅛".

> **The Type I trade-off — this rule runs backwards.** A *smaller* perimeter buys *less*
> reduction, so it makes the clear zone *larger*. Rounding the cap therefore makes the
> neck requirement grow:
>
> | Cap perimeter | Reduction | Clear zone required below the rail |
> |---|---|---|
> | 6¼" milled square | 9/16" | **15/16"** |
> | 5.61" at R ⅜" | 13/32" | **1 3/32"** |
> | 5.28" fully bullnosed | 5/16" | **1 3/16"** |
>
> The neck is drawn **1¼" tall** and inset ⅜" each side, which clears all three however
> the shop eases the cap. **Do not reduce it to 1⅛"** — that is 1/16" short if the cap is
> fully bullnosed. Below the 1¼" neck the built-up post may return to full width.

**8. EXTENSIONS AND RETURNS (§1014.6).** Handrails shall return to a wall, guard or the
walking surface, or shall be continuous to the handrail of an adjacent flight. Where not
continuous between flights, handrails shall extend horizontally not less than 12" beyond
the top riser and continue to slope for the depth of one tread beyond the bottom riser.
**Exc. 1:** handrails within a dwelling unit that is not required to be accessible need
extend only from the top riser to the bottom riser. Where fittings or bendings provide
continuous transition between flights, at winder treads, from handrail to guard, or at the
start of a flight, the height at the fitting may exceed 38" (§1014.2 Exc. 1 and 2).

**9. CLEARANCE (§1014.7).** Clear space between a handrail and a wall or other surface
shall be not less than **1½"**. The handrail and any adjacent surface shall be free of
sharp or abrasive elements.

**10. PROJECTION (§1014.8).** Projections into the required width of the stairway at each
side shall not exceed **4½"** at or below handrail height.

**11. FITTINGS (§1014.5).** Handrails shall not rotate within their fittings.

**12. STRUCTURAL (§1607.9).** Handrails and guards shall be designed to resist a linear
load of **50 plf** (§1607.9.1) and a concentrated load of **200 lb** applied in any
direction at any point along the top (§1607.9.1.1); for one- and two-family dwellings only
the single concentrated load need be applied. Balusters, panel fillers and guard infill
components shall resist a **50 lb** concentrated load on 1 sq ft (§1607.9.1.2). Post and
sub-rail anchorage to stair and floor framing per structural drawings; solid blocking at
every anchorage point. Handrail brackets, where used, at 48" o.c. max.

**13. MATERIALS AND FINISH.** Cap: solid hardwood, species and finish per finish schedule;
kiln dried, 6–8% moisture content. Continuous lengths where possible; splices scarfed or
splined and glued, located clear of turns. All fasteners countersunk and plugged — no
exposed fasteners on any gripping surface. Sand all graspable surfaces smooth; ease all
edges R ⅜" typical per note 6.

## WHAT THE FABRICATOR MUST NOT DRIFT ON

Three numbers carry the compliance. Everything else is dimensional preference.

1. **Cap perimeter ≤ 6¼".** 2" × 1⅛" with the edges eased. Growing either dimension, or
   milling the edges square, walks it out of Type I.
2. **Cap height ≥ 1".** The minimum cross-sectional dimension. 1⅛" as drawn.
3. **Neck ≥ 1¼" tall, inset from the cap sides.** Nothing — post, bracket, baluster, trim —
   may stand proud of the cap within that zone.

## OTHER TYPE I SIZES

Hold even milled dead square (6" perimeter): **2" × 1"** · **1¾" × 1¼"** · **1½" × 1½"**.

Qualify only once the edges are eased (6½" square): **2" × 1¼"** · **2¼" × 1"**. Do not put
either on a drawing without the radius callout.

Note that 2" × 1" sits exactly on the 1" minimum cross-sectional dimension — the same
zero-tolerance trap as the 6¼" perimeter, in the other direction. 2" × 1⅛" avoids both.
