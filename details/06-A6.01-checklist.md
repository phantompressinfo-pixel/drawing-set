# 6/A6.01 — STONE WALL TRANSITION TO METAL STANDING SEAM ROOF — 100% CD checklist

Read of the condition (confirm): anchored stone veneer over a cavity, continuous
insulation and an air/water barrier on a concrete wall; metal standing seam roof
over rigid insulation on a concrete deck framing into that wall below. The detail
is drawn but the entire water-management transition — flashings, terminations,
barrier continuity — is missing.

## Drawing elements to ADD (not shown today)
- [ ] Base flashing, turned up 8" min. above finished roof, hemmed top and bottom
- [ ] Continuous treated blocking / metal sub-girt for base flashing attachment
- [ ] Through-wall flashing at base of veneer, turned down as counterflashing,
      4" min. lap over the base flashing, hemmed drip
- [ ] Shelf / relieving angle at the base of the veneer (or confirm bearing below)
- [ ] Panel end hemmed up 2" min. into a continuous offset cleat
- [ ] Concealed floating clips at each seam
- [ ] High-temp self-adhered underlayment, turned up the wall behind the flashing
- [ ] Slip sheet and cover board
- [ ] Air/vapor barrier over the deck turned up the concrete face and lapped with
      the wall air barrier — the single most important line in this detail
- [ ] Wall insulation running continuous into the roof insulation, no gap at corner
- [ ] Mortar net at the base of the cavity, weeps at 24" o.c. above the flashing
- [ ] Sealant and backer rod at the counterflashing head joint
- [ ] Stone anchor shown with its thermal isolation and barrier penetration sealed
- [ ] Slope arrow, T.O. roof panel elevation target
- [ ] Dimensions: 8" flashing height, 4" counterflashing lap, clearance from roof
      surface to bottom of stone

## Biggest technical issues
1. No flashing at all at the transition — base flashing plus a counterflashing out
   of the veneer is the whole detail.
2. Air and water barrier continuity between roof and wall is undrawn; so is the
   insulation continuity at the corner.
3. Veneer support at this level is unresolved — if the stone doesn't bear here,
   say where it does.

## Verify before issue
- Headwall or sidewall? If the slope runs parallel to the wall this needs step
  flashing and a kickout at the low end — a different detail.
- Roof slope vs. panel profile (hydrokinetic vs. hydrostatic/seam-sealed)
- Snow drift and snow retention
- Clearance, bottom of stone to roof surface (8" min. recommended)
- W10 / R2 tags agree with the assembly schedule
