STAIR NOTES - TREAD & RISER
SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENCE - R-15 - CITY OF ASPEN

1.  CODE BASIS. 2021 IBC AS ADOPTED AND AMENDED BY ASPEN M.C. CH. 8.20. THE CITY DID NOT
    ADOPT THE IRC (8.16.010) - RESIDENTIAL WORK RUNS UNDER THE IBC AS GROUP R-3.

2.  RISER AND TREAD. GROUP R-3 LIMITS ARE 7 3/4" MAX RISER AND 10" MIN TREAD - 1011.5.2
    EXC. 3. BASEMENT-MAIN 7 1/2" / 11". MAIN-UPPER 6 7/8" / 11". BOTH COMPLY.
    ** THE 7 1/2" RISER EXCEEDS THE 7" BASE LIMIT AND STANDS ONLY ON EXC. 3. CITE IT. **

3.  TREAD DEPTH IS MEASURED HORIZONTALLY BETWEEN THE VERTICAL PLANES OF THE FOREMOST
    PROJECTION OF ADJACENT TREADS, AT A RIGHT ANGLE TO THE NOSING - 1011.5.2.

4.  UNIFORMITY. WITHIN EACH FLIGHT, LARGEST TO SMALLEST RISER AND LARGEST TO SMALLEST
    TREAD SHALL NOT VARY MORE THAN 3/8" - 1011.5.4. THE TWO FLIGHTS MAY DIFFER FROM EACH
    OTHER; THE TOLERANCE IS PER FLIGHT.
    ** VERIFY TOP AND BOTTOM RISERS AGAINST ACTUAL FINISH FLOOR THICKNESS AT EACH LEVEL
    BEFORE CUTTING STRINGERS. THIS IS WHERE STAIRS FAIL INSPECTION. **

5.  ALL RISE AND RUN DIMENSIONS ARE TO FINISH SURFACES AND ARE EXCLUSIVE OF CARPET, RUGS
    OR RUNNERS - 1011.5.1.

6.  NOSING PROFILE. EASE THE LEADING EDGE OF EVERY TREAD - CURVATURE OR BEVEL 1/16" MIN TO
    9/16" MAX FROM THE FOREMOST PROJECTION - 1011.5.5. REQUIRED EVEN WHERE THE TREAD DOES
    NOT PROJECT.

7.  NO NOSING PROJECTION IS REQUIRED AT AN 11" TREAD. THE 3/4"-1 1/4" PROJECTION OF
    1011.5.2 EXC. 3 APPLIES ONLY WHERE THE TREAD IS LESS THAN 11". IF THE TREAD IS REDUCED,
    ADD THE PROJECTION, HOLD IT TO 1 1/4" MAX, AND MATCH IT AT EVERY TREAD AND AT THE FLOOR
    EDGE AT THE TOP OF THE FLIGHT - 1011.5.5.1 AND 1011.5.5.2.

8.  RISERS SHALL BE SOLID - 1011.5.5.3. RISER FACE VERTICAL, OR SLOPED UNDER THE TREAD
    ABOVE NOT MORE THAN 30 DEGREES FROM VERTICAL - 1011.5.5.

9.  HEADROOM 80" MIN, MEASURED VERTICALLY FROM THE LINE CONNECTING THE TREAD NOSINGS -
    1011.3. STAIR WIDTH PER PLAN, 36" MIN AT AN OCCUPANT LOAD UNDER 50 - 1011.2. HANDRAIL
    AND GUARD PER THE HANDRAIL DETAIL.

10. STRINGERS, BLOCKING, FASTENING AND ANCHORAGE PER STRUCTURAL. SET TREADS AND RISERS IN
    CONTINUOUS ADHESIVE AND SCREW TO STRINGERS AND BLOCKING; NO EXPOSED FASTENERS ON TREADS.
