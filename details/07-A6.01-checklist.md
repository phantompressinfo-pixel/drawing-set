## Biggest technical issue
The deck bears on the wall with insulation above the deck and (per the original)
insulation only on the interior face below — an uninsulated slab edge / thermal
bridge. Carrying the XPS down the exterior face of the wall 2'-0" min. below the
deck closes it and protects the membrane at the same time.

- Membrane type + spec section number

- Intensive vs. extensive — media depth and saturated dead load
- Soil retention at the backfill / gravel interface
- Drain + overflow locations shown on the roof plan
