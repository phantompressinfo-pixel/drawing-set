WOOD RISER & HANDRAIL NOTES  -  5/A6.31
SECTION CUT ACROSS THE STAIR  -  MAIN TO UPPER LEVEL

THIS VIEW GOVERNS WIDTH, CLEARANCE AND THE TREAD BEARING AT THE WALL.
RISE AND RUN ARE GOVERNED BY 4/A6.31 AND THE STAIR SECTION AT GRID 6-7.

WIDTH AND CLEARANCES

1.  CLEAR STAIR WIDTH PER PLAN. MEASURE BETWEEN FINISH FACES, NOT BETWEEN FRAMING.
    VERIFY AFTER WALL FINISH IS ON AND WITH THE HANDRAIL IN PLACE - NOT BEFORE.

2.  MAINTAIN 1 1/2" MIN CLEAR BETWEEN THE HANDRAIL GRIP AND THE ADJACENT WALL FINISH FOR
    THE FULL LENGTH OF THE RAIL. HOLD THIS AT TRIM, BASE, AND AT ANY WALL-MOUNTED ITEM.

3.  THE HANDRAIL AND ITS SUPPORTS MAY PROJECT 4 1/2" MAX INTO THE REQUIRED STAIR WIDTH AT
    EACH SIDE.

4.  NOTHING ELSE TO ENCROACH ON THE STAIR WIDTH WITHIN THE RUN - NO TRIM, WALL SCONCES,
    SWITCHES, THERMOSTATS, ART HOOKS OR SHELVING. COORDINATE WITH INTERIORS AND ELECTRICAL.

WALL, BLOCKING AND TREAD BEARING

5.  STAIR WALL FRAMING AND ALL BLOCKING PER STRUCTURAL. PROVIDE CONTINUOUS SOLID BLOCKING
    FOR THE FULL LENGTH OF THE HANDRAIL AND AT EVERY TREAD BEARING POINT.

6.  TREADS TO BEAR ON A CONTINUOUS LEDGER OR HOUSED STRINGER AT THE WALL. DO NOT BEAR
    TREADS ON GYPSUM BOARD, TRIM OR SHIMS ALONE.

7.  SCRIBE TREADS AND RISERS TIGHT TO THE FINISHED WALL. NO GAPS AND NO CAULK-FILLED JOINTS
    AT THE TREAD ENDS.

8.  WALL FINISH TO BE COMPLETE AND CONTINUOUS BEHIND THE HANDRAIL AND BEHIND THE STAIR
    BEFORE TREADS ARE SET. DO NOT STOP FINISH AT THE STAIR AND PATCH LATER.

9.  CONFIRM ALL BLOCKING IS IN PLACE AND RECORDED BEFORE THE WALL IS CLOSED. NO ELECTRICAL
    BOXES, LOW VOLTAGE DEVICES OR PIPING WITHIN THE BLOCKING ZONE.

HANDRAIL

10. HANDRAIL HEIGHT 34" MIN / 38" MAX ABOVE THE LINE CONNECTING THE TREAD NOSINGS, AS
    DIMENSIONED. HOLD ONE HEIGHT FOR THE FULL FLIGHT.

11. HANDRAIL ON ONE SIDE MINIMUM. WHERE A RAIL OCCURS ON BOTH SIDES, HOLD BOTH AT THE SAME
    HEIGHT AND THE SAME PROFILE.

12. HANDRAIL CONTINUOUS FOR THE FULL FLIGHT, RETURNING TO THE WALL AT BOTH ENDS. THE RAIL
    SHALL NOT ROTATE OR DEFLECT IN ITS FITTINGS.

13. SEE 3/A6.31 FOR HANDRAIL PROFILE AND DIMENSIONS.

MATERIALS AND WORKMANSHIP

14. ALL WOOD KILN DRIED AND ACCLIMATED TO INTERIOR CONDITIONS A MINIMUM OF 72 HOURS BEFORE
    INSTALLATION. DO NOT INSTALL FINISH WOODWORK UNTIL THE BUILDING IS DRIED IN.

15. COUNTERSINK AND PLUG ALL FASTENERS. NO EXPOSED FASTENERS ON THE HANDRAIL, THE TREADS OR
    ANY SURFACE WITHIN REACH. SAND ALL EXPOSED SURFACES AND EDGES SMOOTH.

16. SEE INTERIORS DRAWINGS FOR ALL FINISH MATERIALS, SPECIES, STAIN AND SHEEN.

17. PROTECT ALL FINISHED WORK UNTIL SUBSTANTIAL COMPLETION. DO NOT USE FINISHED TREADS AS
    CONSTRUCTION STAIRS.

GENERAL

18. FIELD VERIFY ALL DIMENSIONS AND CONDITIONS BEFORE FABRICATION. NOTIFY THE ARCHITECT OF
    ANY DISCREPANCY BEFORE PROCEEDING.

19. SUBMIT SHOP DRAWINGS FOR THE STAIR AND HANDRAIL ASSEMBLY - PROFILES, JOINTS, SPLICES,
    RETURNS, TREAD BEARING AND ATTACHMENT - FOR REVIEW PRIOR TO FABRICATION.

20. THE RISER DIMENSION SHOWN IS FOR REFERENCE ONLY. RISER HEIGHT AND COUNT ARE SET BY THE
    STAIR SECTION AT GRID 6-7 AND ITS RISER TABLE. DO NOT SCALE.

KEYNOTES

A.  WOOD HANDRAIL - SEE 3/A6.31 FOR PROFILE AND DIMENSIONS.
B.  STAIR WALL - SEE INTERIORS FOR FINISH. FINISH CONTINUOUS BEHIND THE RAIL, NOTE 8.
C.  ENGINEERED WOOD TREAD, SCRIBED TIGHT TO THE FINISHED WALL - NOTE 7.
D.  CONTINUOUS SOLID WOOD BLOCKING PER STRUCTURAL - NOTES 5, 6 AND 9.
E.  1 1/2" MIN CLEAR, GRIP TO WALL FINISH - NOTE 2.
