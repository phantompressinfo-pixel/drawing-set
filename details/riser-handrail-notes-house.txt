WOOD RISER & HANDRAIL NOTES  -  5/A6.31
MAIN - UPPER LEVEL

GENERAL

1.  FIELD VERIFY ALL DIMENSIONS AND EXISTING CONDITIONS BEFORE FABRICATION. NOTIFY THE
    ARCHITECT OF ANY DISCREPANCY BEFORE PROCEEDING.

2.  DIMENSIONS ARE TO FINISH FACES UNLESS NOTED OTHERWISE.

3.  SUBMIT SHOP DRAWINGS FOR THE STAIR AND HANDRAIL ASSEMBLY - PROFILES, JOINTS, SPLICES,
    RETURNS AND ATTACHMENT - FOR REVIEW PRIOR TO FABRICATION.

4.  PROVIDE A HANDRAIL SAMPLE, 12" MIN LENGTH, IN THE SPECIFIED SPECIES AND FINISH FOR THE
    ARCHITECT'S APPROVAL BEFORE RUNNING THE ORDER.

MATERIALS AND WORKMANSHIP

5.  ALL WOOD KILN DRIED AND ACCLIMATED TO INTERIOR CONDITIONS A MINIMUM OF 72 HOURS BEFORE
    INSTALLATION. DO NOT INSTALL FINISH WOODWORK UNTIL THE BUILDING IS DRIED IN AND
    CONDITIONED.

6.  RUN THE HANDRAIL IN THE LONGEST LENGTHS AVAILABLE. SCARF OR SPLINE ALL JOINTS, GLUE AND
    CLAMP. LOCATE JOINTS AWAY FROM TURNS, TRANSITIONS AND ENDS.

7.  COUNTERSINK AND PLUG ALL FASTENERS. NO EXPOSED FASTENERS ON THE HANDRAIL OR ON ANY
    SURFACE WITHIN REACH.

8.  SAND ALL EXPOSED SURFACES AND EDGES SMOOTH. NO SPLINTERS, TOOL MARKS, GLUE SQUEEZE-OUT
    OR TELEGRAPHING OF FASTENERS.

9.  THE HANDRAIL SHALL NOT ROTATE OR DEFLECT IN ITS FITTINGS. SHIM AND SECURE AS REQUIRED.

BLOCKING AND ATTACHMENT

10. PROVIDE CONTINUOUS SOLID WOOD BLOCKING PER STRUCTURAL AT ALL HANDRAIL, GUARD AND TREAD
    BEARING POINTS. DO NOT ATTACH TO GYPSUM BOARD, TRIM OR FINISH MATERIAL ALONE.

11. COORDINATE BLOCKING WITH THE FRAMER AND CONFIRM IT IS IN PLACE BEFORE THE WALL IS
    CLOSED. PHOTOGRAPH AND RECORD BLOCKING LOCATIONS PRIOR TO COVER.

12. NO ELECTRICAL BOXES, LOW VOLTAGE DEVICES OR PIPING WITHIN THE BLOCKING ZONE. COORDINATE
    ALL ROUGH-IN IN THIS WALL WITH BLOCKING BEFORE INSTALLATION.

HEIGHT AND LAYOUT

13. HANDRAIL HEIGHT 34" MIN / 38" MAX MEASURED VERTICALLY FROM THE LINE CONNECTING THE TREAD
    NOSINGS. HOLD ONE HEIGHT, UNIFORM, FOR THE FULL FLIGHT.

14. SET HANDRAIL LAYOUT FROM THE FINISHED NOSING LINE, NOT FROM SUBFLOOR OR STRINGER. ALLOW
    FOR ACTUAL FINISH FLOOR THICKNESS AT BOTH LEVELS.

15. HANDRAIL TO BE CONTINUOUS FOR THE FULL FLIGHT AND TO RETURN TO THE WALL OR GUARD AT BOTH
    ENDS.

FINISHES AND COORDINATION

16. SEE 3/A6.31 FOR HANDRAIL PROFILE AND DIMENSIONS. SEE 4/A6.31 FOR TREAD AND RISER.

17. SEE INTERIORS DRAWINGS FOR ALL FINISH MATERIALS, SPECIES, STAIN AND SHEEN. WALL FINISH
    TO RUN CONTINUOUS AND UNINTERRUPTED BEHIND THE HANDRAIL.

18. PROTECT ALL FINISHED WORK UNTIL SUBSTANTIAL COMPLETION.

KEYNOTES

A.  WOOD HANDRAIL - SEE 3/A6.31 FOR PROFILE AND DIMENSIONS.
B.  WOOD GUARD WALL - SEE INTERIORS FOR FINISH.
C.  ENGINEERED WOOD TREAD - SEE 4/A6.31.
D.  CONTINUOUS SOLID WOOD BLOCKING PER STRUCTURAL - SEE NOTES 10 THROUGH 12.
E.  LINE CONNECTING TREAD NOSINGS - DATUM FOR HANDRAIL HEIGHT, NOTE 13.
