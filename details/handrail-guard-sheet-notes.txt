HANDRAIL / GUARD NOTES - INTERIOR STAIR
2021 IBC AS ADOPTED AND AMENDED BY CITY OF ASPEN M.C. CH. 8.20. GROUP R-3.

1.  CODE BASIS. ALL WORK PER THE 2021 INTERNATIONAL BUILDING CODE AS ADOPTED AND
    AMENDED BY ASPEN M.C. CH. 8.20. THE 2021 IRC IS NOT ADOPTED BY THE CITY
    (ASPEN M.C. 8.16.010); IBC 101.4.11 AS AMENDED DELETES ALL IRC REFERENCES AND
    APPLIES THIS CODE TO ONE- AND TWO-FAMILY DWELLINGS AND TOWNHOUSES.

2.  HANDRAILS REQUIRED ON EACH SIDE OF STAIRWAYS PER IBC 1011.11; STAIRWAYS WITHIN
    DWELLING UNITS PERMITTED TO HAVE A HANDRAIL ON ONE SIDE ONLY. HANDRAILS SHALL BE
    ADEQUATE IN STRENGTH AND ATTACHMENT PER 1607.9 (1014.1).

3.  HEIGHT. HANDRAIL HEIGHT ABOVE TREAD NOSINGS SHALL BE UNIFORM, 34" MIN TO 38" MAX
    (1014.2). WHERE THE TOP OF THE GUARD SERVES AS THE HANDRAIL AT THE OPEN SIDE OF A
    STAIR IN GROUP R-3, TOP OF GUARD 34" MIN TO 38" MAX ABOVE THE LINE CONNECTING THE
    LEADING EDGES OF THE TREADS (1015.3 EXC. 3). GUARDS AT LANDINGS AND FLOORS 36" MIN
    ABOVE THE WALKING SURFACE (1015.3 EXC. 1); 42" WHERE THE EXCEPTIONS DO NOT APPLY.
    ** CONFIRM GUARD HEIGHT WITH ASPEN BUILDING DEPT. BEFORE ISSUE - SEE COVER. **

4.  GUARDS REQUIRED ALONG OPEN-SIDED WALKING SURFACES, INCLUDING STAIRS AND LANDINGS,
    LOCATED MORE THAN 30" MEASURED VERTICALLY TO THE FLOOR OR GRADE BELOW AT ANY POINT
    WITHIN 36" HORIZONTALLY TO THE EDGE OF THE OPEN SIDE - 1015.2 AS AMENDED BY ASPEN
    M.C. 8.20.020.

5.  OPENING LIMITATIONS (1015.4). GUARDS SHALL NOT ALLOW PASSAGE OF A 4" SPHERE FROM
    THE WALKING SURFACE TO THE REQUIRED GUARD HEIGHT. WITHIN DWELLING UNITS, GUARDS ON
    THE OPEN SIDES OF STAIRS SHALL NOT ALLOW PASSAGE OF A 4 3/8" SPHERE (EXC. 6).
    TRIANGULAR OPENINGS FORMED BY RISER, TREAD AND BOTTOM RAIL SHALL NOT ALLOW PASSAGE
    OF A 6" SPHERE (EXC. 2). FROM 36" TO 42" ABOVE THE WALKING SURFACE, 4 3/8" SPHERE
    (EXC. 1).

6.  GRASPABILITY (1014.3). TYPE I (1014.3.1): CIRCULAR 1 1/4" TO 2" DIA.; NON-CIRCULAR
    PERIMETER 4" TO 6 1/4" WITH 2 1/4" MAX AND 1" MIN CROSS-SECTIONAL DIMENSION.
    TYPE II (1014.3.2), REQUIRED WHERE PERIMETER EXCEEDS 6 1/4": GRASPABLE FINGER
    RECESS BOTH SIDES, BEGINNING WITHIN 3/4" MEASURED VERTICALLY FROM THE TALLEST
    PORTION OF THE PROFILE, ACHIEVING 5/16" MIN DEPTH WITHIN 7/8" BELOW THE WIDEST
    PORTION, THAT DEPTH CONTINUING NOT LESS THAN 3/8" TO A LEVEL NOT LESS THAN 1 3/4"
    BELOW THE TALLEST PORTION; WIDTH ABOVE THE RECESS 1 1/4" MIN TO 2 3/4" MAX. EDGES
    0.01" MIN RADIUS. THE 2 1/2" x 2" CAP SHOWN HAS A PERIMETER OF APPROX. 9" AND IS
    DETAILED AS TYPE II.

7.  CONTINUITY (1014.4). GRIPPING SURFACES SHALL BE CONTINUOUS, WITHOUT INTERRUPTION BY
    NEWEL POSTS OR OTHER OBSTRUCTIONS. WITHIN A DWELLING UNIT, INTERRUPTION BY A NEWEL
    POST AT A TURN OR LANDING IS PERMITTED (EXC. 1), AS IS A VOLUTE, TURNOUT, STARTING
    EASING OR STARTING NEWEL OVER THE LOWEST TREAD (EXC. 2). BRACKETS OR BALUSTERS
    ATTACHED TO THE BOTTOM OF THE RAIL THAT DO NOT PROJECT BEYOND ITS SIDES WITHIN
    1 1/2" OF THE BOTTOM ARE NOT OBSTRUCTIONS (EXC. 3); FOR EACH 1/2" OF PERIMETER
    ABOVE 4", THE 1 1/2" MAY BE REDUCED BY 1/8". FOR THE PROFILE SHOWN: 9" - 4" = 5";
    5 / 0.5 = 10 x 1/8" = 1 1/4" REDUCTION; 1 1/2" - 1 1/4" = 1/4" REQUIRED CLEAR.

8.  EXTENSIONS AND RETURNS (1014.6). HANDRAILS SHALL RETURN TO A WALL, GUARD OR THE
    WALKING SURFACE, OR BE CONTINUOUS TO THE HANDRAIL OF AN ADJACENT FLIGHT. WHERE NOT
    CONTINUOUS BETWEEN FLIGHTS, EXTEND HORIZONTALLY 12" MIN BEYOND THE TOP RISER AND
    CONTINUE TO SLOPE FOR THE DEPTH OF ONE TREAD BEYOND THE BOTTOM RISER. WITHIN A
    DWELLING UNIT NOT REQUIRED TO BE ACCESSIBLE, HANDRAILS NEED EXTEND ONLY FROM THE
    TOP RISER TO THE BOTTOM RISER (EXC. 1). FITTINGS OR BENDINGS PROVIDING CONTINUOUS
    TRANSITION MAY EXCEED THE 38" MAX HEIGHT (1014.2 EXC. 1 AND 2).

9.  CLEARANCE (1014.7). CLEAR SPACE BETWEEN HANDRAIL AND WALL OR OTHER SURFACE 1 1/2"
    MIN. HANDRAIL AND ADJACENT SURFACE FREE OF SHARP OR ABRASIVE ELEMENTS.

10. PROJECTIONS (1014.8). PROJECTIONS INTO THE REQUIRED STAIR WIDTH AT EACH SIDE SHALL
    NOT EXCEED 4 1/2" AT OR BELOW HANDRAIL HEIGHT.

11. FITTINGS (1014.5). HANDRAILS SHALL NOT ROTATE WITHIN THEIR FITTINGS.

12. STRUCTURAL (1607.9). HANDRAILS AND GUARDS DESIGNED TO RESIST A 50 PLF LINEAR LOAD
    (1607.9.1) AND A 200 LB CONCENTRATED LOAD APPLIED IN ANY DIRECTION AT ANY POINT
    ALONG THE TOP (1607.9.1.1); FOR ONE- AND TWO-FAMILY DWELLINGS ONLY THE SINGLE
    CONCENTRATED LOAD NEED BE APPLIED. BALUSTERS, PANEL FILLERS AND INFILL COMPONENTS
    TO RESIST A 50 LB CONCENTRATED LOAD ON 1 S.F. (1607.9.1.2). POST AND SUB-RAIL
    ANCHORAGE PER STRUCTURAL; SOLID BLOCKING AT ALL ANCHORAGE POINTS. BRACKETS, WHERE
    USED, AT 48" O.C. MAX.

13. MATERIALS AND FINISH. CAP: SOLID HARDWOOD, SPECIES AND FINISH PER FINISH SCHEDULE,
    KILN DRIED 6-8% M.C. CONTINUOUS LENGTHS WHERE POSSIBLE; SPLICES SCARFED OR SPLINED
    AND GLUED, LOCATED AWAY FROM GRIP TRANSITIONS AND CLEAR OF TURNS. ALL FASTENERS
    COUNTERSUNK AND PLUGGED - NO EXPOSED FASTENERS ON GRIPPING SURFACES. SAND ALL
    GRASPABLE SURFACES SMOOTH; EASE ALL EDGES R 1/8" TYP.
