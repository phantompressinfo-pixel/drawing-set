# Handrail / Guard Sheet Notes — Interior Stair, Single-Family

Companion to `handrail-guard-detail.svg`. Written for a **City of Aspen** parcel:
2021 IBC as adopted and amended, Group R-3, interior stair, handrail integral with
the guard at the open side.

Drop notes 1–13 on the sheet as the general note block; keynotes **A–G** are on the
detail itself.

---

## GENERAL NOTES

**1. CODE BASIS.** All work per the 2021 International Building Code as adopted and
amended by City of Aspen Municipal Code Ch. 8.20. The 2021 IRC is **not adopted** by
the City — Aspen M.C. §8.16.010: *"The International Residential Code, 2021 Edition,
will not be adopted."* IBC §101.4.11 as amended by §8.20.020: *"All references to the
International Residential Code (IRC) within this code shall be deleted and the
requirement of this code as it pertains to one- and two-family dwellings and
townhouses shall apply."* Occupancy is Group R-3. Do not cite IRC R311 or R312 on
this sheet.

> **Zoning is not the controlling code here, but it confirms the occupancy.** R-15
> (Moderate-Density Residential, §26.710.050(a)) permits a detached residential dwelling,
> a duplex, or two detached dwellings as of right (§26.710.050(b)(1)–(3)). Those are IBC
> **Group R-3**, which is what makes the R-3 exceptions cited throughout these notes
> available. A triplex or fourplex — permitted in R-15 only at 100% deed-restricted
> affordable housing, §26.710.050(b)(4) — would be **Group R-2**, and the exceptions then
> read "within individual dwelling units in Group R-2"; re-check §1015.3 and §1015.4 if the
> use changes. Title 26 contains no handrail or guard design provisions of its own.
>
> One R-3 exception carries a story limit: **§1015.3 Exc. 1** (36" guards at landings and
> floors) applies to Group R-3 **not more than three stories above grade in height**.
> R-15's 25 ft height limit (§26.710.050(d)(7)) makes that a non-issue on a normal house,
> but confirm the story count — basements below grade are not stories above grade.
> §1015.3 Exc. 2 and Exc. 3, which govern the guard at the open side of the stair, carry no
> story limit.

**2. HANDRAILS REQUIRED.** Stairways shall have handrails on each side and comply
with §1014; stairways within dwelling units and spiral stairways are permitted to
have a handrail on one side only (§1011.11). Handrails serving flights of stairways
shall be adequate in strength and attachment in accordance with §1607.9 (§1014.1).

**3. HEIGHT.** Handrail height, measured above stair tread nosings, shall be uniform,
not less than **34"** and not more than **38"** (§1014.2). Where the top of the guard
serves as the handrail at the open side of a stair in a Group R-3 occupancy, the top
of the guard shall be not less than 34" and not more than 38" measured vertically
from the line connecting the leading edges of the treads (§1015.3 Exc. 3). Guards at
landings and floors: not less than **36"** above the adjacent walking surface for
Group R-3 not more than three stories above grade (§1015.3 Exc. 1). Base requirement
absent those exceptions is 42" (§1015.3).

> ⚠ **VERIFY BEFORE ISSUE.** This office's City of Aspen sets have been drawn with
> **42"** guards. Aspen M.C. §8.20.020 amends §1015.3 only by *adding* Exception 9
> (surfaces adjacent to hot tubs, spas and pools), so base Exceptions 1–3 remain
> available and 36"/34–38" is defensible for R-3. Confirm the intended height with
> the Aspen Building Department, then dimension **one** height consistently across
> plans, sections, elevations and this detail.

**4. GUARDS — WHERE REQUIRED.** Per §1015.2 as amended by Aspen M.C. §8.20.020:
*"Guards shall be located along open-sided walking surfaces, including mezzanines,
equipment platforms, aisles, stairs, ramps, landings and adjacent to hot tubs, spas,
and pools that are located more than 30 inches (762 mm) measured vertically to the
floor or grade below at any point within 36 inches (914 mm) horizontally to the edge
of the open side. Guards shall be adequate in strength and attachment in accordance
with Section 1607.9."*

**5. OPENING LIMITATIONS (§1015.4).** Required guards shall not have openings that
allow passage of a **4"** sphere from the walking surface to the required guard
height. Exceptions applicable here:
- Exc. 6 — within individual dwelling units in Group R-2 and R-3, guards on the open
  sides of stairs shall not allow passage of a **4⅜"** sphere.
- Exc. 2 — triangular openings at the open side of a stair formed by the riser, tread
  and bottom rail shall not allow passage of a **6"** sphere.
- Exc. 1 — from 36" to 42" above the walking surface, guards shall not allow passage
  of a **4⅜"** sphere.

**6. GRASPABILITY (§1014.3).** Required handrails shall comply with §1014.3.1 (Type I)
or provide equivalent graspability; §1014.3.2 governs Type II.
- **Type I (§1014.3.1):** circular cross section 1¼"–2" outside diameter. Non-circular:
  perimeter 4"–6¼", maximum cross-sectional dimension 2¼", minimum 1". Edges 0.01" min
  radius.
- **Type II (§1014.3.2):** required where perimeter exceeds 6¼". Graspable finger
  recess on **both** sides. Recess begins within **¾"** measured vertically from the
  tallest portion of the profile; achieves a depth of not less than **5/16"** within
  **7/8"** below the widest portion; that depth continues for not less than **3/8"** to
  a level not less than **1¾"** below the tallest portion. Width above the recess:
  **1¼" min to 2¾" max**. Edges 0.01" min radius.

The cap on this detail is 2" wide × 2¼" tall in two bands — **¾" top + 1½" recess** —
with the recess **⅜" deep each side** (neck 1¼"), on a built-up **2"** post (¾" + ½" + ¾"
plies) flush with the cap. Its perimeter is **8½"**, so Type I is not available and the
profile is Type II. Three limits are drawn as hard callouts: top of recess **¾" max**
below the top of the profile, bottom of recess **1¾" min** below it, and nothing
projecting beyond the sides of the grip within **⅜"** of the bottom of the rail.

**7. CONTINUITY (§1014.4).** Handrail gripping surfaces shall be continuous, without
interruption by newel posts or other obstructions. Exceptions applicable within a
dwelling unit: interruption by a newel post at a turn or landing (Exc. 1); a volute,
turnout, starting easing or starting newel over the lowest tread (Exc. 2). Brackets or
balusters attached to the bottom surface of the handrail that do not project
horizontally beyond the sides of the handrail within 1½" of its bottom are not
obstructions (Exc. 3); for each ½" of handrail perimeter above 4", the 1½" vertical
clearance may be reduced by ⅛".

> **Calc for this profile:** 8½" − 4" = 4½"; 4½ ÷ ½" = 9 increments; 9 × ⅛" = 1⅛"
> reduction; 1½" − 1⅛" = **⅜"**. Nothing may project beyond the sides of the rail
> within ⅜" of its bottom. The 2" post is flush with the 2" cap, so nothing projects
> at all. **A post wider than the cap fails this** — see the profile check below.

**8. EXTENSIONS AND RETURNS (§1014.6).** Handrails shall return to a wall, guard or the
walking surface, or shall be continuous to the handrail of an adjacent flight. Where
not continuous between flights, handrails shall extend horizontally not less than 12"
beyond the top riser and continue to slope for the depth of one tread beyond the
bottom riser. **Exc. 1:** handrails within a dwelling unit that is not required to be
accessible need extend only from the top riser to the bottom riser. Where fittings or
bendings provide continuous transition between flights, at winder treads, from
handrail to guard, or at the start of a flight, the height at the fitting may exceed
38" (§1014.2 Exc. 1 and 2).

**9. CLEARANCE (§1014.7).** Clear space between a handrail and a wall or other surface
shall be not less than **1½"**. The handrail and any adjacent surface shall be free of
sharp or abrasive elements.

**10. PROJECTION (§1014.8).** Projections into the required width of the stairway at
each side shall not exceed **4½"** at or below handrail height.

**11. FITTINGS (§1014.5).** Handrails shall not rotate within their fittings.

**12. STRUCTURAL (§1607.9).** Handrails and guards shall be designed to resist a
linear load of **50 plf** (§1607.9.1) and a concentrated load of **200 lb** applied in
any direction at any point along the top (§1607.9.1.1); for one- and two-family
dwellings only the single concentrated load need be applied. Balusters, panel fillers
and guard infill components shall resist a **50 lb** concentrated load on an area of
1 sq ft (§1607.9.1.2). Post and sub-rail anchorage to stair and floor framing per
structural drawings; provide solid blocking at every anchorage point. Handrail
brackets, where used, at 48" o.c. max.

**13. MATERIALS AND FINISH.** Cap: solid hardwood, species and finish per finish
schedule; kiln dried, 6–8% moisture content. Continuous lengths where possible;
splices scarfed or splined and glued, located away from grip transitions and clear of
turns. All fasteners countersunk and plugged — no exposed fasteners on any gripping
surface. Sand all graspable surfaces smooth; ease all edges R ⅛" typical.

## PROFILE CHECK — THE SKETCHED PROFILE vs §1014.3.2 AND §1014.4

Profile as sketched: 2" wide × 2¼" tall, bands **1⅛" + 1⅛"**, recess **⅜"** deep each
side (neck 1¼"), on a built-up **2¼"** post of three ¾" plies, ½" from post face to neck
face. Perimeter **8½"**.

| Requirement | Required | As sketched | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Type II applies | perimeter > 6¼" | 8½" | applies |
| Recess depth, each side | 5/16" min | ⅜" | OK |
| Depth continues for | ⅜" min | 1⅛" | OK |
| Depth reaches below top of profile | 1¾" min | 2¼" | OK |
| Width above recess | 1¼"–2¾" | 2" | OK |
| **Recess begins below top of profile** | **¾" max** | **1⅛"** | **fails** |
| **Projection beyond sides of grip, §1014.4 Exc. 3** | **none within ⅜" of rail bottom** | **post ⅛" proud each side, at 0"** | **fails** |

Two failures, both one-line moves:

1. **Bands ¾" + 1½"** instead of 1⅛" + 1⅛". The cap stays 2" × 2¼", the neck stays 1¼",
   and the recess then runs ¾" to 2¼" below the top — inside both limits at once.
2. **Post 2" max** — ¾" + ½" + ¾" plies instead of three ¾" plies — so it is flush with
   the cap. At an 8½" perimeter the §1014.4 Exc. 3 zone reduces from 1½" to ⅜", and the
   2¼" post breaks that plane right at the underside of the rail: fingers curling under
   the grip land on its shoulder.

The detail is drawn to both fixes.

### The Type I alternative — no recess at all

Type I (§1014.3.1) is available here, and your top band already meets it. The rule for a
non-circular rail: **perimeter 4"–6¼", maximum cross-sectional dimension 2¼", minimum 1",
edges 0.01" min radius.** Drawn in `handrail-detail-type-i.svg`.

**The idea that makes it work:** the handrail is the **2" × 1⅛" cap only**. What sits
below the bottom of the rail is a sub-rail, not part of the grip, and is not counted in
the perimeter. Measured as one 2" × 2¼" section the same profile is 8½" and Type II is
forced; measured as the cap alone it is Type I.

| | Required | 2" × 1⅛" cap |
|---|---|---|
| Perimeter | 4"–6¼" | 6¼" milled square · 5.61" at R ⅜" · 5.28" fully bullnosed |
| Max cross-sectional dimension | 2¼" | 2" |
| Min cross-sectional dimension | 1" | 1⅛" |
| Edge radius | 0.01" min | R ⅜" |

**What then controls the neck.** §1014.4 Exc. 3 reduces its 1½" zone by ⅛" per ½" of
perimeter above 4" — so a *smaller* perimeter is *stricter*, and rounding the cap makes
the neck requirement grow:

| Cap perimeter | Clear zone required below the rail |
|---|---|
| 6¼" (milled square) | 15/16" |
| 5.61" (R ⅜") | 1 3/32" |
| 5.28" (fully bullnosed) | 1 3/16" |

Draw the neck **1¼" tall, inset ⅜" each side** and it clears all three. A 1⅛" neck is
1/16" short if the cap is fully bullnosed. Below the neck the built-up post may return
to any width — 2¼" (three ¾" plies) as drawn. Overall top of rail to top of post: **2⅜"**.

Other Type I sizes that hold even milled square: **2" × 1"**, **1¾" × 1¼"**, **1½" × 1½"**
(all 6" perimeter). **2" × 1¼"** and **2¼" × 1"** are 6½" square and qualify only once the
edges are eased — do not draw them without a radius callout.

Everything else on this sheet is unchanged by the switch: height, continuity, returns,
clearance, guard openings, loads.
