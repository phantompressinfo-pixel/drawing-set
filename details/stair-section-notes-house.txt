GENERAL

1.  FIELD VERIFY ALL FLOOR-TO-FLOOR DIMENSIONS AND FINISH FLOOR THICKNESSES AT EVERY LEVEL
    BEFORE CUTTING STRINGERS OR ORDERING MATERIAL.

2.  ALL RISERS IN A FLIGHT SHALL BE EQUAL. THE TOP AND BOTTOM RISERS ARE THE ONES THAT DRIFT
    - THEY MUST BE SET FROM FINISH FLOOR TO FINISH FLOOR, NOT SUBFLOOR TO SUBFLOOR.

3.  THE TWO FLIGHTS ARE SEPARATE RUNS AND ARE NOT REQUIRED TO MATCH EACH OTHER. EACH FLIGHT
    MUST BE UNIFORM WITHIN ITSELF.

4.  HEADROOM 6'-8" MIN, MEASURED VERTICALLY FROM THE LINE CONNECTING THE TREAD NOSINGS, AS
    DIMENSIONED. VERIFY AT SOFFITS, BEAMS, DUCTS AND LIGHT FIXTURES ABOVE EACH FLIGHT.

5.  SUBMIT STAIR SHOP DRAWINGS SHOWING ACTUAL FIELD DIMENSIONS, RISER AND TREAD COUNTS,
    STRINGER LAYOUT, LANDING FRAMING AND HANDRAIL ATTACHMENT. DO NOT FABRICATE UNTIL
    REVIEWED.

STRUCTURE AND COORDINATION

6.  STAIR OPENING FRAMING, HEADERS, STRINGER HANGERS AND LANDING SUPPORT PER STRUCTURAL.
    COORDINATE ALL BEARING AND HANGER LOCATIONS BEFORE FRAMING.

7.  PROVIDE CONTINUOUS SOLID BLOCKING IN ALL STAIR AND GUARD WALLS FOR HANDRAIL AND GUARD
    ATTACHMENT. CONFIRM IN PLACE BEFORE WALLS ARE CLOSED.

8.  COORDINATE MECHANICAL, ELECTRICAL AND PLUMBING RUNS WITH THE STAIR SOFFIT AND WITH THE
    6'-8" HEADROOM. NOTHING TO ENCROACH.

9.  COORDINATE STAIR AND SOFFIT LIGHTING WITH INTERIORS. NO FIXTURE TO REDUCE REQUIRED
    HEADROOM.

10. SEAL AND INSULATE AT THE CRAWLSPACE PER THE ENVELOPE DETAILS AND ENERGY COMPLIANCE
    DOCUMENTS.

HANDRAILS, GUARDS AND FINISHES

11. HANDRAIL CONTINUOUS FOR THE FULL LENGTH OF EACH FLIGHT, RETURNING TO WALL, GUARD OR
    NEWEL AT BOTH ENDS. HOLD ONE HEIGHT PER FLIGHT.

12. GUARDS AT ALL OPEN SIDES AND AT THE OPEN EDGE OF EACH FLOOR OPENING. SEE THE HANDRAIL
    AND GUARD DETAILS.

13. SEE INTERIORS DRAWINGS FOR ALL FINISH MATERIALS, SPECIES, STAIN AND SHEEN THROUGHOUT.

14. PROTECT THE STAIR AND ALL FINISHED WORK UNTIL SUBSTANTIAL COMPLETION. DO NOT USE
    FINISHED TREADS AS CONSTRUCTION STAIRS.
