COMMENT 3 - AIR BARRIER CONTINUITY AND AIR SEALING
============================================================================

Provide details and plans showing a continuous air barrier enclosing the
entire conditioned space. Identify the designated air barrier material and
show its continuity at all transitions, intersections and penetrations. Air
barrier and insulation installation shall comply with 2021 IECC Table
R402.4.1.1. Confirm the whole building air leakage rate and the test method.

RESPONSE:

The set now designates the air barrier explicitly, carries that designation
through every wall type and every detail, and documents each component row
of Table R402.4.1.1.

a.  Designated barrier named. General note 20 on A5.00 has been rewritten to
    name the designated continuous air barrier by zone: closed cell spray foam
    at all framed walls and roofs, full depth of all cavities, 1-1/2" minimum
    for the air barrier function; foundation waterproofing on the exterior face
    of the concrete below grade; Monolithic Membrane 6125 fully adhered to the
    structural slab at the green roof; and a self-adhered membrane lapped 3"
    minimum onto both surfaces at every transition between zones. The note
    states that the four zones lap and seal to one another to form one unbroken
    plane.

b.  Conflicting note corrected. General note 21 previously stated that the
    closed cell spray foam was not the designated barrier, which contradicted
    every air sealing detail in the set and read on site as permission not to
    seal the foam. It has been rewritten. The self-adhered weather resistant
    barrier, gypsum board, nail base panel, rigid insulation and vinyl blanket
    are identified as supplementary air sealing. The WRB is identified as the
    water barrier and as the surface the transition membranes lap onto, with all
    sheathing joints taped behind it.

c.  Wall types brought into agreement. Assemblies W3, W4, W10, W11, W13 and
    W14 on A5.00 now carry "DESIGNATED AIR BARRIER" on the closed cell spray
    foam line, matching note 20 and the details. The framing callout is
    corrected to 2x6 throughout, since R-34 is not achievable in a 2x4 cavity.
    Wall type W15, the garage side wall of the mechanical chase, has been added;
    that wall was drawn in the details without a type.

d.  Air sealing details. Sheet G2.09 carries twelve details, each with its
    own note block and code citation: crawl space slab at foundation, green roof
    edge, return corner, roof at ridge, eave and sill plate, roof to wall at
    abutting roof, garage and dwelling separation with the mechanical chase, low
    slope membrane roof, window and door rough opening, and partition at
    exterior wall. The unvented roof assembly detail carries the IRC R806.5
    citation. The partition at exterior wall detail is new and is called out
    from the plans at the stair shaft and at each interior wall landing on an
    exterior wall.

e.  Envelope diagrams. The thermal envelope sections on G2.08 show the
    barrier continuous and keynoted around the full section. At the lower level
    floor the label reading "ground as insulation" has been replaced with four
    callouts naming the concrete slab, the Class I vapor retarder under the
    slab, the foundation waterproofing on the exterior face, and the perimeter
    rigid insulation continuous to the footing. A note confirms the concrete
    wall carries continuity from the exterior waterproofing to the under-slab
    retarder, so there is no break at that joint.

f.  Keynote legend. An air sealing keynote legend has been added, listing
    keynotes 1 through 12 against the detail that resolves each one, so every
    flag on the sections and plans resolves to a drawing.

g.  Table R402.4.1.1. A compliance schedule listing all sixteen component
    rows of 2021 IECC Table R402.4.1.1 has been placed on G2.07, each row paired
    with the assembly, detail and note that satisfies it.

h.  City of Aspen spray foam details. The City of Aspen "Air Sealing Details
    Using Spray Foam" are reproduced in the set. A note confirms that closed
    cell spray foam is the designated air barrier on this project per note 20,
    that the sealant scope shown on those details is required in addition to the
    foam rather than in place of it, and that the self-adhered WRB per the wall
    types is substituted where those details show housewrap.

i.  Testing. Whole building air leakage will be verified by blower door test
    at not more than 1.5 ACH50 per the City of Aspen amendment to 2021 IECC
    R402.4.1.2. Duct leakage will be tested per R403.3.5. Both reports will be
    provided to the building department before the final inspection.

Sheets revised:  G2.07 · G2.08 · G2.09 · A5.00

COMMENT 4 - SOLAR READY — 2021 ASPEN ENERGY CODE R404.5
============================================================================

Show compliance with City of Aspen 2021 Energy Code Section R404.5, Solar
Ready. Provide a solar ready zone of not less than 250 square feet, a
dedicated space in the main service panel reserved for future photovoltaic
interconnection, and a conduit pathway from the panel to the solar ready
zone. Show the fire service access pathways and setbacks required by IFC
1205.2.1, and confirm the structure will accept the future array.

RESPONSE:

A new electrical sheet and a revised roof plan have been added. No
photovoltaic system is installed under this permit; the dwelling is solar
ready only.

a.  Solar ready zone. One continuous zone measuring 22'-0" by 12'-6", 275
    square feet, is shown on the south facing slope of the main roof — see
    5/A2.14 and the roof plan. R404.5 permits the zone to be a single area; the
    option to divide it into areas of not less than 80 square feet with a
    minimum dimension of 5'-6" has not been used. The zone is free of vents,
    chimneys and other obstructions, and 12" minimum clear is maintained to the
    skylight curbs each side.

b.  Fire service access. One 3'-0" clear access pathway is provided from
    eave to ridge on the slope carrying the zone per IFC 1205.2.1.1, located
    alongside the zone rather than through it. An 18" clear setback is
    maintained below the ridge per IFC 1205.2.1.2. Neither the pathway nor the
    ridge setback is counted toward the 250 square foot minimum, per the
    exception in R404.5.

c.  Main service panel. A dedicated two-pole space is reserved at the load
    end of the busbar, at the opposite end from the 200A main, and is
    permanently labeled "FOR FUTURE SOLAR ELECTRIC." The space will not be used
    for any other load. See the panel schedule, 2/E___. Future interconnection
    will be made per NEC 705.12(B)(3)(2), load-end busbar connection.

d.  Conduit pathway. A 1" minimum empty raceway with a pull string runs from
    a junction box within 2'-0" of the main service panel to a junction box
    within 3'-0" of the solar ready zone, and from there to a capped roof
    penetration sleeve at the zone. Both ends are labeled "FOR FUTURE SOLAR
    ELECTRIC." The run contains no more than the equivalent of four quarter
    bends between pull points. See the riser diagram, 1/E___.

e.  Working space. NEC 110.26 working space, 3'-0" deep by 30" wide by 6'-6"
    high, and 110.26(E) dedicated equipment space are shown at the main service
    panel on the enlarged plan, 3/E___.

f.  Envelope penetration. Where the conduit crosses the thermal envelope it
    is sealed to the designated air barrier per general notes 20 through 30 on
    A5.00, so the solar ready pathway does not compromise the air barrier
    addressed in comment 3.

g.  Structure. The roof structure over the solar ready zone is designed for
    an additional ___ psf dead load for a future photovoltaic array. See the
    structural drawings.
