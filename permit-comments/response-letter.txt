COMMENT 3 - AIR BARRIER CONTINUITY AND AIR SEALING
============================================================================

Provide details and plans showing a continuous air barrier enclosing the
entire conditioned space. Identify the designated air barrier material and
show its continuity at all transitions, intersections and penetrations. Air
barrier and insulation installation shall comply with 2021 IECC Table
R402.4.1.1. Confirm the whole building air leakage rate and the test method.

RESPONSE:

The air barrier is now designated by zone, and that designation carries
through every wall type and every detail. Table R402.4.1.1 is documented row
by row.

a.  Air barrier designated by zone: closed cell spray foam at framed walls
    and roofs, foundation waterproofing below grade, Monolithic Membrane 6125 at
    the green roof, self-adhered membrane at the transitions between them.
    See:  Gen. note 20 A5.00

b.  Note 21 rewritten. It previously stated the spray foam was not the
    designated barrier.
    See:  Gen. note 21 A5.00

c.  "DESIGNATED AIR BARRIER" added to the spray foam line. Framing corrected
    to 2x6.
    See:  W3, W4, W10, W11, W13, W14 / A5.00

d.  Wall type added for the garage side of the mechanical chase.
    See:  W15 / A5.00

e.  Nine air sealing details, each with its note block and code citation.
    The unvented roof assembly cites IRC R806.5.
    See:  1–9 / G2.09

f.  Dwelling separation wall at exterior wall added and called out from the
    plans.
    See:  9 / G2.09

g.  "Ground as insulation" replaced with the slab, under-slab vapor
    retarder, foundation waterproofing and perimeter rigid insulation.
    See:  1 / G2.08

h.  Air sealing keynote legend, keynotes 1–11 against the detail that
    resolves each, and a legend for the designated air barrier line.
    See:  G2.08

i.  Roof and ceiling assemblies at R-60 min. per Table R402.1.2, Climate
    Zone 7.
    See:  2–6 / G2.09

j.  Table R402.4.1.1 compliance schedule, all sixteen component rows.
    See:  G2.07

k.  Blower door test at 1.5 ACH50 max. per the Aspen amendment to
    R402.4.1.2; duct leakage per R403.3.5. Reports before final inspection.
    See:  Gen. notes G2.07

Sheets revised:  G2.07 · G2.08 · G2.09 · A5.00

1205.2.1, and confirm the structure will accept the future array.
