Stops it

01 R-30 at the roof assemblies Stops it
WhereDetails 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 / G2.09
As drawnDetails 3 and 5 call “SPRAY FOAM ALL CAVITIES, R-30 MIN.” and “CLOSED CELL SPRAY FOAM AT UNDERSIDE OF SLAB, R-30 MIN.” at roof and ceiling assemblies. Details 2, 4 and 6 carry no R-value at all.
Should beR-30 is the Climate Zone 7 wall value. The ceiling value is R-60 — 2021 IECC Table R402.1.2. Where it appears at a wall (detail 8, at the rough opening) R-30 is correct; everywhere it points at a roof it is half the required value. R402.2.2 does let you use R-30 for a ceiling without attic space where the assembly will not take the full depth, but only for the lesser of 500 sq ft or 20% of the insulated ceiling area — not across the whole roof. Either raise the callouts, or declare a UA alternative or performance path on G2.07 and cite the report. Closed cell foam at R-60 is roughly 9″ to 10″, which a 2x12 rafter bay takes; this is likely just the wall note copied onto the roof details.
CLOSED CELL SPRAY FOAM, FULL DEPTH OF ALL RAFTER BAYS, R-60 MIN. DESIGNATED AIR BARRIER, IN CONTINUOUS CONTACT W/ THE SHEATHING

Fix before it goes

05 The gypsum callout lost its thickness Fix
WhereDetail 7 / G2.09
As drawn“X GYPSUM BOARD AT GARAGE SIDE PER IRC R302.6, SEAL AT BASE”
Should beThe thickness and the word TYPE dropped out. As it reads it specifies nothing. IRC Table R302.6 requires 1/2″ minimum from the residence and attics, and 5/8″ Type X where habitable space occurs above the garage. Specifying 5/8″ Type X covers both cases and saves the question.
5/8" TYPE X GYPSUM BOARD AT GARAGE SIDE PER IRC TABLE R302.6. TAPE AND SEAL ALL JOINTS. SEAL AT BASE.
06 The unvented attic has no code citation Fix
WhereDetail 4 / G2.09
As drawn“CONDITIONED ATTIC (UNVENTED)” with no reference, and no R-value on the ridge foam.
Should beIRC R806.5 is what makes an unvented attic legal, and it is nowhere in the set. Your assembly satisfies it — air-impermeable insulation in direct contact with the underside of the sheathing — but the drawing has to say so. Add a note block under the detail.
1. UNVENTED ATTIC ASSEMBLY PER IRC R806.5. AIR-IMPERMEABLE INSULATION IS APPLIED IN DIRECT CONTACT W/ THE UNDERSIDE OF THE STRUCTURAL ROOF SHEATHING, FULL DEPTH, W/ NO GAP OR SEPARATION. 2. NO VENTILATION OPENINGS OCCUR IN THIS ASSEMBLY. SOFFITS SHALL NOT BE VENTED. 3. CLOSED CELL SPRAY FOAM, R-60 MIN. — SEE FINDING 01.
07 The crawl space has no citation and no conditioning Fix
WhereDetail 1 / G2.09
As drawnSlab, Class I vapor retarder, waterproofing and blanket insulation are all named. Nothing says the crawl space is unvented or how it is conditioned.
Should beThe crawl space is inside the thermal envelope in both sections, so it is an unvented crawl space and IRC R408.3 applies. R408.3.2 requires one of four conditioning methods; the drawing has to pick one and the mechanical sheet has to carry it.
1. UNVENTED CRAWL SPACE PER IRC R408.3. NO VENTILATION OPENINGS OCCUR. 2. PROVIDE CONTINUOUSLY OPERATED MECHANICAL EXHAUST AT 1 CFM PER 50 SQ FT OF CRAWL SPACE FLOOR AREA, OR CONDITIONED AIR SUPPLY AT THE SAME RATE, PER IRC R408.3.2. SEE MECHANICAL.
08 The chase is an open void with no firestopping Fix
WhereDetail 7 / G2.09
As drawn“MECHANICAL CHASE OPEN VOID BETWEEN WALLS”
Should beA continuous vertical void running the full height of the building between the garage and the dwelling. IRC R302.11 requires firestopping in concealed spaces of stud walls at the ceiling and floor levels and at 10 ft intervals. If ducts run in this chase and cross into the garage, R302.5.2 applies as well.
FIRE BLOCKING AT EACH FLOOR LINE AND AT 10'-0" MAX. VERTICALLY, PER IRC R302.11. DUCTS IN THE GARAGE AND DUCTS PENETRATING THIS SEPARATION SHALL BE 26 GA MIN. SHEET METAL W/ NO OPENINGS INTO THE GARAGE, PER IRC R302.5.2.
