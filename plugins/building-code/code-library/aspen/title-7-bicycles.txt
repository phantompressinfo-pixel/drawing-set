===== Chapter 7.04. - BICYCLE REGULATIONS


===== Sec. 7.04.010. - Application for registration.
Each application for a bicycle registration or for a transfer thereof, shall be made
 to the Police Department on a form approved by the Chief of Police. Such application
 shall set forth the name and address of the owner, a description of the bicycle, the
 name of the manufacturer, and the serial number appearing on the frame of the bicycle.
 (Code 1971, § 6-1; 
Ord. No. 25-1977
, § 1)

===== Sec. 7.04.020. - Registration fee.
No fee shall be required for registration of bicycles pursuant to this Section.
 (Code 1971, § 6-2; 
Ord. No. 25-1977
, § 1)

===== Sec. 7.04.030. - Issuance of registration decal.
Upon application, the Police Department shall engrave an identification number on
 the bicycle frame and issue a registration decal for each bicycle.
 (Code 1971, § 6-3; 
Ord. No. 25-1977
, § 1)

===== Sec. 7.04.040. - Renewal of registration.
No renewal of bicycle registration shall be necessary.
 (Code 1971, § 6-4; 
Ord. No. 25-1977
, § 1)

===== Sec. 7.04.050. - Responsibility on transfer of title of bicycle.
When the bicycle has been sold or ownership transferred, the bicycle may be registered
 in the name of the person to whom such sale or transfer was made.
 (Code 1971, § 6-5; 
Ord. No. 25-1977
, § 1)

===== Sec. 7.04.060. - Records to be kept by the police department.
The Police Department shall keep a record of the registration of all bicycles.
 (Code 1971, § 6-6; 
Ord. No. 25-1977
, § 1)

===== Sec. 7.04.070. - Replacement of stolen, lost or mutilated decals.
Whenever an issued registration decal is lost, stolen or mutilated, a replacement
 (registration) decal may be obtained from the Police Department.
 (Code 1971, § 6-7; 
Ord. No. 25-1977
, § 1)
