EAD OFFICE HUB — BUTTON IMAGES
==============================

Two sets. Pick by what colour the page behind them is:

  *-navy.png    navy button — use on a WHITE or light page
  *-white.png   white button — use on a NAVY or dark section

All have transparent rounded corners, so they sit on any background
without a white box around them.

SIZES (rendered at 3x so they stay sharp on any screen)
  card-*.png     960 x 570 px   — displays at 320 x 190
  button-*.png   453 x 168 px   — displays at 151 x 56

PUTTING ONE IN GOOGLE SITES
  1. Insert > Image > Upload, and pick the PNG
  2. Drag it to the size you want (three cards fit across a page)
  3. Click it, then the LINK icon in the little toolbar
  4. Paste the Drive folder or document address

That's it — the whole image becomes the button.

LAYING OUT THE CARDS
  Sites content is about 1000px wide. Three cards across, with a gap,
  is about 320px each — the size they are drawn at. Sites will snap
  them into a row for you.

FONT
  Montserrat, the same font to set in the Sites theme so the buttons
  match the rest of the page.

COLOURS
  Navy   #022049  (Pantone 282 C)
  Text on white cards  #022049 heading, #5C6672 description
